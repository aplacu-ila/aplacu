# Creative Asset Lifecycle

Aplacu treats design production as a lifecycle rather than a one-off image export.

```text
BRIEF
  ↓
CONCEPT
  ↓
DESIGN
  ↓
PROOF
  ↓
CLIENT REVIEW
  ↓
REVISION
  ↓
APPROVED
  ↓
LOCKED
  ↓
PRODUCTION
  ↓
DIGITAL / PRINT
```

## Versioning

- `v0.x` = working concepts
- `v1.0` = approved master
- `v1.x` = approved adaptations
- `*-print` = print production output
- `*-digital` = digital output

## Why this exists

In digital design, a revision can be undone. In physical print production, an approved file can become irreversible once production starts. Proofing and approval therefore belong to the production workflow, not as an afterthought.
