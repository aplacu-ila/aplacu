# APLACU Brand Relaunch

## Identity

**APLACU**  
**Creative Production Studio**

> Design is an evolving asset.

## Production Philosophy

Aplacu treats creative work as an asset lifecycle: brief, concept, design, proof, approval, production, and adaptation.

## Core Principle

**Create once. Adapt everywhere.**

One approved master can become digital, social, marketplace, packaging, promotional, and print outputs.

## Visual Identity

The Aplacu mark is a modular house-like symbol representing a creative structure built in layers. The official vector mark lives at `brand/aplacu-mark.svg`.

## Brand Story

The deeper meaning behind the name **AP · LA · CU** is intentionally reserved for a dedicated brand-story session.
